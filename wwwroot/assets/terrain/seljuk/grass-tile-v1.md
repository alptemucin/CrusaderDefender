## Kullanılan üretim promptu

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square grass terrain texture for a cartoon fantasy isometric tower defense game. Create one 1024x1024 opaque square tile viewed EXACTLY straight down, no perspective, no isometric diamond baked in. The renderer will project this square into an isometric diamond. Soft painterly cartoon grass, short clumps of three tapered grass blades, broad rounded patches, tasteful confident dark outlines, mid-green sage and moss palette with small warm lime highlights and dark teal shadow patches. Grass blades lie visually low and do not form upright objects. Even diffuse lighting. Clearly hand-painted game art, legible at small scale, richer than flat checkerboard, no photoreal grain. Seamless on ALL four edges, consistent overall color at edges, no border, no tile grid, no transparent margin, no bare dirt, no stones, no flowers, no props, no buildings, no text. Plenty of calm empty grassy area between small sparse clumps, avoid dense busy detail. Ground texture only.
